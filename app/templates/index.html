Frequently asked questions

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring web content so AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Google's AI Overviews — can extract, summarize, and cite it. It overlaps with traditional SEO but emphasizes direct-answer paragraphs, clean heading hierarchy, schema.org markup, inline citations, content freshness, and entity coverage rather than backlinks and keyword density.

How is AEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO optimizes for ranking on a search-results page; AEO optimizes for being quoted inside an AI-generated answer. AI engines parse for structured direct answers (1–3 sentences, no hedging), valid JSON-LD schema, factual citations, and broad semantic coverage of a topic. Pages can rank #1 on Google and still be invisible to ChatGPT or Perplexity if the structure isn't extractable.

How does AEGIS score my content?

AEGIS runs 6 structural checks on every page: (1) Direct Answer — does the first paragraph give a 40–80 word declarative answer without hedge phrases? (2) Heading Hierarchy — exactly one H1 with no level skips? (3) Schema Markup — valid JSON-LD with the right @type for the page's intent? (4) Citations — inline sources to authoritative domains? (5) Freshness — explicit publish/update date within the last 18 months? (6) Entity Coverage — enough relevant entities named to be semantically complete? Each check returns a sub-score and the AEO score is their weighted sum, capped at 100.

What is query fan-out and why does it matter for AI search?

When you ask an AI engine a question, the engine internally rewrites your query into 10–15 narrower sub-queries across different intents — comparative ("X vs Y"), feature-specific, use-case, trust-signal, how-to, and definitional — then retrieves and synthesizes evidence for each. If your content only answers the literal target query and ignores the fan-out, the engine cites whichever competitor does cover those intents. AEGIS generates the same kind of fan-out with an LLM and checks which of your competitors' likely sub-queries you actually answer.

Does AEGIS require signup or payment?

No. Three scans per IP per day are free with no account required. Paid plans unlock unlimited scans, AI-generated rewrites that re-pass every failed check, scheduled site-wide audits, GEO citation tracking, and API access.

Which AI search engines does AEGIS optimize for?

AEGIS targets the structural signals shared by every major AI answer engine: ChatGPT (Browse), Perplexity, Claude with web access, Google AI Overviews, Bing Copilot, You.com, and Meta AI. The 6 structural checks and the LLM fan-out are engine-agnostic — fixing them improves citation odds across all of them simultaneously.

Can I paste raw HTML or just URLs?

Both. Use the URL tab for any published page, or paste raw HTML or plain text into the "Raw text / HTML" tab to scan unpublished drafts before they ship. The same 6 structural checks and the optional query fan-out run on either input.
