How to Pick an AI Writing Tool for SEO

The best AI writing tool for SEO is one that scores content against live SERPs, suggests semantic terms from real ranking pages, and exports clean schema markup. Surfer, Clearscope, and MarketMuse all do this; pick by budget and team size.

What to Look For

Look for real-time SERP analysis, semantic term clustering, and a clean editor. Skip tools that only count keywords.

Real-time SERP Scoring

The tool should fetch top-ranking pages and score your draft against them as you write.

Semantic Term Suggestions

Good tools surface entities and related terms, not just keyword density numbers.

Pricing Tiers

Most tools charge per seat. Solo plans run $50 to $90 a month. Team plans start near $150.

Free Trials

Every major tool offers a free trial. Use it on one real article before paying.
